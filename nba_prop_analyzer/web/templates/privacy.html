Privacy Policy

Version {{ legal.version }}

The short version: we collect only what we need to run accounts and the free-tier limits. There are no ads, no advertising or analytics trackers, no session recording, and we do not sell your data.

1. Who we are

matchupedge.com (“matchupedge”, “we”) is operated by {% if legal.operator %}{{ legal.operator }}{% else %}the operator of this site{% endif %}.{% if legal.contact_email %} For anything in this policy, contact {{ legal.contact_email }}.{% endif %}

2. What we collect

  • Account details — your name and email address, your plan (free or paid), and counters for the free-tier allowance (how many analyses you have used and when the current allowance window started). Your password is handled by our authentication provider, which stores only a hash of it; we never see or store the plain-text password.
  • Sign-up record — that you confirmed you are {{ min_age }} or older, which version of the Terms and this policy you accepted and when, and whether you chose to receive product updates.
  • Anonymous visitors — to enforce one free analysis per day we store a salted, one-way hash of your IP address together with the date you last used it. We do not store your IP address in that record, and those records are deleted after about a week.
  • The analyses you run — the player, opponent, prop and line you enter are used to produce your result. We do not keep a history of them against your identity.
  • Hosting logs — our hosting provider automatically records standard request information (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and operations. We do not use it to profile visitors.

3. Cookies and tracking

We set a single cookie, and only when you sign in. It keeps you logged in, is signed and HttpOnly, and is strictly necessary for the site to work, so we do not show a cookie banner. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, fingerprinting, or session-recording tools. If that changes we will update this policy first and use a privacy-respecting option.

We host our fonts, team logos and player images ourselves, so your browser does not contact Google or ESPN when you load a page.

4. Who else handles your data

  • Supabase — account authentication and our database.
  • Brevo — delivery of account emails (confirmation and password reset).
  • Our hosting provider — runs the site and keeps the logs described above.

The “League wire” headlines are fetched by our server from public news feeds and link out to the publishers. If you click one, that publisher’s own privacy policy applies. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

5. Why we use it (legal bases)

To provide the service you asked for (your account and analyses); for our legitimate interests in security, preventing abuse and enforcing usage limits; with your consent where you gave it (for example product updates); and where the law requires.

6. How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. The hashed anonymous-usage records are deleted after about a week. Hosting logs follow our provider’s retention schedule.
