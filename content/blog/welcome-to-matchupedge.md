Most prop-betting tools in this space are built around a confident-sounding
conclusion — a lock, a pick of the day, a tout's win rate. That's a
different product than the one we set out to build.

matchupedge runs a real statistical matchup analysis for any player, prop,
and opponent: pace, opponent defense by shot zone, schedule fatigue,
ball-pressure profile, and more, all pulled from this season's real data.
Every projection comes with a Component Breakdown that names the single
factor that actually moved the number, in plain language, with the real
number behind it.

## Why this matters

A number without a reason is just an opinion in disguise. If a tool tells
you a player is projected for 27.5 points and won't say why, you have no
way to judge whether that reasoning holds up against what you already
know about the matchup. Show the "why," and a bettor can actually use
their own judgment instead of just trusting a stranger's confidence.

That's the whole idea behind this blog, too — we'll use this space to
walk through how specific factors work, what changed in the model and
why, and how to actually read a Component Breakdown before you bet. No
picks here either. Just the reasoning.
